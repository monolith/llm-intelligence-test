**Brief conflict, r08.** X30's assigned wording — "The rise of 1924 is the measure of it" — contains
the word *measure*, which the same brief forbids "anywhere, in any connection." The verbatim rule
was given priority, so the sentence stands exactly as briefed and the word occurs once, in its
non-instrument sense. X28 also required him to report a condemnation of "the glassware" without
using the words glass, pipette or measure; it is rendered as "the station's testing apparatus,"
which keeps the near-tie date phrase intact and the banned vocabulary out.

## Places the brief could not be satisfied exactly

1. **r09 — telling date vs. F021.** The brief sets the voice at "sixty-seven in 1955" and also
   assigns him the 1958 loan of the weigh book to Ivy. A single 1955 document cannot carry a 1958
   event, so the piece is dated 1955 with a short postscript dated 1958. No other device was
   available without dropping one of the two.
2. **r09 — F021's second half.** "Hazel had it after him" is a post-1962 custody fact. It is
   carried as his stated intention ("after me it is Hazel's"), which is all a living narrator can
   say.
3. **r10 — F017's end date.** She ran the factory 1941–1962; writing in 1958 she can only give the
   start. Stated as "seventeen years now."
4. No other item was impossible. All near-tie values (X35 16.27 c.c., X36 the first of May 1898,
   X37 five dozen, X38 the five-and-a-quarter denominator, X40 forty-four million, X41 nineteen
   thousand six hundred dollars, X42 the twelfth of July 1923, X43 eleven pipettes, X44 his nephew)
   are reproduced character-for-character from the brief.

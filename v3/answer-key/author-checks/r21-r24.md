## Notes for the editor

1. **r22's arithmetic is internally inconsistent by design.** ¶5 gives the recorded average as 3.61
   and the deficiency as 0.19 (both correct); ¶11 gives the post-1923 average as "three eighty-five"
   (X90). A model that reconciles them will catch the near-tie; that appears to be the intent, but
   flagging it in case the coverage audit assumed r22 would carry only one of the two.
2. **r21 calls Ivy Keddie "the granddaughter"** (canon-correct) while also asserting X87, that she
   was Hazel Brant's first cousin once removed. Both statements stand as briefed; the brief assigned
   him no error on the grandparent relation, so the correct value was used there.
3. Nothing in the four briefs was impossible to satisfy as written.

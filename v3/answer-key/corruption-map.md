# Corruption Map, v3 (SECRET — never show to the system under test)

Twenty-four narrators (r01–r24). For each: who they are, what slice of the eight-story history
they hold, their reliability profile, and the exact list of planted errors with the wording the
author must reproduce.

**Design rules.**
1. Every planted error is unique to one narrator — **except** the designated **near-tie pairs**
   (twenty-two of them), where exactly **two** narrators carry the identical wrong value, a
   straight source count gives a tie or a false majority, and only a quoted document, arithmetic,
   or one narrator's internal consistency breaks it (§ Near-tie pairs).
2. Every scored fact appears correctly in **≥2 narrators**, or is **derivable by arithmetic**, or
   is **settled by a quoted document** (§ Recoverability index).
3. **Abstention poles** (marked ⌀) are not errors. They are the paired unsupported claims that
   make A01–A10 unsettleable. A narrator carrying one is not "wrong"; the solver is wrong if it
   picks a side.
4. **Decoy theories** (three: watering, theft, bog hay) are designed shared beliefs, not
   uniqueness breaches. Each is carried by at least two narrators and refuted by a document and
   by arithmetic.
5. The corpus contains **no** claim that is true and unrecoverable, and **no** scored fact whose
   only carrier also carries a planted error about it.

## r01 — Vira Toland, a Hessel Bottom patron's daughter (b. 1912), recorded at Ostrey Hollow, 1979

**Slice.** Village memory of Ostrey Hollow: the station as a building and a routine, the 1919
circular as the families experienced it, the 1925 hearing as gossip, the Keddies and the Brants
as people. Nothing documentary, nothing of the college, nothing of Alder Corners.

**Reliability profile.** Excellent on texture and on the moral weather of the hamlet — the shame

## What went wrong

**Section A is where the run lost the batch (7/19).** The three narratives are fluent and well
sourced, but they are thin on exactly the facts the key checks. Story 1 carries the founding and
Article VII and stops: no Effie Loomis, no Wilbur Teague, no Selby Vose, no station-gain line.
Story 2 gets the station, the invoice and the $77.93 sum right but never describes how a test was
actually made (composite sample, fixed charge, percent on the neck) and never says the shortfall
was flat month by month. Story 3 spends its budget on the 1908 hay committee — a Batch 2 story —
so Keddie himself is reduced to one sentence: no test book, no summary leaf, no 1911 interval, no
1919 circular, no death in 1934.

The costly structural choice is that the run's real analytical content sits in Sections B and C.
The 0.19 shortfall, the 3.61/3.80 pair, the percent-graduated neck, the station-gain line and
Selby Vose's parentage are all stated somewhere in the sheet — just never inside Section A, where
the key forbids credit from travelling in. Six of the twelve lost A items are lost that way.

second cousins with a real derivation, Gideon Vose with the nephew story rejected, and a clean

item A08 — not asserted either, so no penalty, just the point), and at B4 it says no kinship is

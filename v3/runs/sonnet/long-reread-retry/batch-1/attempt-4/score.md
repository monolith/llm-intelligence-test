## What went wrong

founding / station-opening / the 1908 wet-hay committee. The third is A4 material, which belongs to
Batch 2, so it spent a whole narrative earning almost nothing here while A3 — Keddie and the test
book — went untold. A3 lost the test book itself, the summary leaf, the nine weeks of 1911, the
1919 circular and Keddie's 1925 statement, and his death in 1934: six of seven items simply absent.

**It filed Section A facts in Sections B and C.** The station-gain ledger line (A1.6), the flat
0.19-in-every-month signature (A2.5) and the 3.61/3.80 summary-leaf pair (A3.3) are all stated
correctly — but only inside C1. Selby Vose (A1.5) appears only at B3/B4. Credit does not travel into
Section A from B–G, so four otherwise-earned items were lost to filing location alone.

**It never found the pipette measurement.** C1(a) asserts "the sources never give a raw calibration
number for the pipettes" and back-derives 5.25–5.26 percent from 0.19/3.61 instead of 16.72 ÷ 17.6 =
0.95. It reached the right downstream numbers (3.80, 0.19, 76,000 lb) by a different route, but the
keyed answer for (a) — 95 percent of true, short 5 percent — is not there.

**Two planted errors swallowed.** $19,600 as the loss, adopted over the correct $19,000 and with the
correct figure explicitly dismissed as a transcription slip; and Selby Vose as manager from 1911
rather than 1913, asserted twice. −1 each.

**C2 declined outright.** The largest-patron figures are in the corpus (Brant's 1910 year: 200,000 lb
at a recorded 3.42). C2 is not an abstention item, so the decline scores 0 for all three sub-points.

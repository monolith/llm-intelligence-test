## What went wrong

**Section A is where the batch was lost.** The run chose its own eight-episode partition and spent
its three narratives on the founding, the station's opening and purchase, and the 1902–08 hay
committee. The third narrative is A4 material — Hessel Bottom and the patrons — which is Batch 2's
checklist, so it earned almost nothing here, while A3 (Keddie and the test book) went essentially
untold: no test book, no summary leaf, no 1911 interval, no 1919 circular content, no 1934 death.
Six of the seven A3 items are simply absent.

**Facts filed in the wrong section don't travel.** Three A items — the station-gain ledger line
(A1.6), the flat 0.19 in every month (A2.5) and the 3.61/3.80 summary leaf (A3.3) — are all stated
correctly by the run, but only inside Section C. Credit never travels into Section A from B–G, so
all three are zero. That is the key's stated, accepted cost of the rule, and this sheet paid it
three times.

**17.6 c.c. never appears anywhere in the sheet.** The run reports the invoice and its $77.93 total
correctly but never recovers the marked capacity of the pipettes, which costs A2.4 and, more
seriously, makes C1(a) unanswerable: it declares "the sources give no raw calibration number for the
pipette" (false — the 1958 measurement gives 16.72 against 17.6) and back-derives 5.25% from
0.19 ÷ 3.61 instead of 16.72 ÷ 17.6 = 0.95. Wrong denominator, wrong answer.

**C1(d) is the NT-8 cascade, exactly as the key's diagnostics predict.** It adopts $19,600, calls
the true $19,000 "a rounding or transcription slip," and produces 19.4 percent instead of one fifth.

**C2 was refused outright** on the claim that no individual patron's 1910 figures exist. Brant's
200,000 lb at 3.42 is in the corpus. C2 is not an abstention item; a refusal scores zero, so 3
points went uncontested.

**Corruption:** −3. Selby Vose manager "from 1911" (twice), the $19,600 loss, and 67,000 lb of
butterfat. The 67,000 is the notable one: rather than flagging it as an error, the run invented a
provenance for it ("the patrons' own 1925 arbitration estimate… a contemporaneous guess… not meant
to be the same number"), which is absorption, not hedging.

## What it did well

No gullibility deductions. B4 declines A01 cleanly ("cannot be determined from the sources") while
still saying what the institutional relationship was. B1 resolves granddaughter by a named,
dated in-document self-correction and rejects the great-niece corruption. B3 rejects the nephew
story with three named sources. The 1908 hay finding is reported as the committee's finding and
explicitly marked as later shown mistaken — no decoy adopted. It kept $77.93 over $77.39, 1896 over
1895 for Keddie's start, six farms over seven, 1 May 1897 over 1898, and Larrow Green over Ashlin
for the borrowed glass. B2's second half (A08, full vs half siblings) was not asserted — it was
simply never raised, which costs the point but takes no deduction.

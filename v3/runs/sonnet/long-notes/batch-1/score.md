## What went wrong

**The 16.27 cascade.** The run took the planted pipette figure as fact and built C1 on it: a 7.6
percent shortfall instead of 5 percent, and, compounded with the planted 44,000,000 lb of milk and
Rundle's $19,600, wrong answers at C1(a), (c) and (d). It then noticed that its own two routes to
"how wrong" (7.6 percent proportional vs 0.19 of a point) do not reconcile, and reported both
rather than treating the mismatch as the defeater it is. The one surviving Math point in C1 is
3.80 / 0.19, which it reached by source-convergence rather than arithmetic. C2 fails the same way:
a flat 0.19 applied to a 3.42 herd gives 380 lb where the proportional 0.95 gives 360, and $95.00
where the 1910 price of 24 cents gives $86.40. It also asserted the 3.85 post-repair average, but
explicitly as an unresolved loose end, so that one is a hedge and not a deduction.

**Section A is thin because of what it chose to write.** It submitted three narratives covering the
founding, the Tarnet glass, and the 1908 wet-hay committee — the third of which is A4 material
(batch 2) and earns nothing here. A3 scores zero: Keddie's twenty-nine seasons, the test book and
its summary leaf, the nine weeks of 1911, Orra as Emmet Brant's sister, the 1919 circular, the 1925
dismissal — all of it either absent or stated only in Sections B and C, from which credit does not
travel into A. Several near-misses are structural: it knows 0.19, the burette and the 1911 interval,
but files them under Math.

**Relationships.** B1 is clean, including the use of Falke's own appended correction. B2 gets the
second-cousin derivation but mishandles the abstention: it correctly declines the full/half sibling
question, then says half siblings would make Hazel and Ivy "half second cousins" — the key requires
the statement that it does not change the answer. B3 names the father correctly but supplies its own
significance (inherited standing, careless signing) instead of the Article VII link. B4 is the
expensive one: it answers "None, by the best evidence," treating Duncan Keddie's family denial as
decisive, which is exactly the trap A01 sets.

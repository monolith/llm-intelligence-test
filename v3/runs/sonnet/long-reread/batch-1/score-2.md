## What went wrong

**Section A is starved by filing.** The run wrote three narratives covering the founding/purchase,
the 1908 hay committee, and the 1911 interval — so it spent one of its three slots on A4 material
(Hessel Bottom and the patrons), which is not scored in this batch, and never told the Keddie story,
the Article VII consequence, or the crate's defect. Worse, the facts that would have carried A1.6,
A2.5 and A3.3 (the station-gain line, the flat 0.19 in every month, the 3.80/3.61 summary leaf) are
all stated correctly — but in Section C, where the key forbids credit from travelling into A. That
alone is three points thrown away on filing rather than knowledge.

**The pipette is never measured.** 16.72 and 17.6 appear nowhere on the sheet. Without them the run
cannot answer C1(a) — it substitutes the ledger's "five and a quarter per cent," which is the gain
against credited fat, not the instrument's shortfall — and it loses A2.4's 17.6 marking. It reaches
0.19 and 3.80 only by quoting the 1958 bulletin, not by deriving them, so C1(b) and C1(c) are
credited on the numbers alone.

**Two planted errors swallowed.** $19,600 is asserted as the money figure at C1(d), with the correct
$19,000 explicitly dismissed as a transcription error — the run derives the right answer in its own
flag and then rejects it. Selby Vose is made manager from 1911 twice. Elsewhere the run hedges
corruption well ($77.39/$77.93, the weigh book's start year, great-niece, nephew, the hay verdict),
so these two are the exceptions rather than a pattern.

**C2 declined outright** — Brant's 1910 year is determinable (200,000 lb at 3.42), and the run had
Brant's weigh book in hand; it lost three points to a decline where the key wanted arithmetic. That
is the batch's single largest avoidable loss.

**B derivations are thin.** B1 gets "granddaughter" from an author's self-correction but never
derives the parentage; B3 names Gideon correctly but gives the wrong significance (inherited
authority rather than Article VII making a wrong test cost money); B4 abstains correctly but never
says what the sources do establish about the alleged cousinship.

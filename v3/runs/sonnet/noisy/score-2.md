## Section A — points lost (14)

theory" and "the 1919 water letter" for the inspector's circuit. Strawn's material therefore lives

choice costs the whole of A6.

a solver that arbitrated superbly — it broke eleven near-tie pairs by document and arithmetic and
absorbed only one planted error in forty thousand words — and then lost fourteen reconstruction

no room for the state inspector, and the key does not let Section F pay Section A's bill. The other

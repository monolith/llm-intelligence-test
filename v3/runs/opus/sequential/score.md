reconstructs and arbitrates well — every near-tie pair in the corpus is named and broken, all three
decoys are refuted with specific defeaters, and the arithmetic closes on itself throughout — but it

sheet *knows and states correctly* in Sections B–G — Rosalie as daughter, the weigh book's 1897
start, Brant's 1910 year, the $19,000, the 40,000,000 lb — but never files inside the eight
reconstructions. That is a filing loss, not a recall loss, and it concentrates in **A4**
(2 of 6), the story whose evidentiary material the sheet routed almost entirely into Sections C
and D.

## Every lost point, one line each

**Section A — 7 lost**

- **A2.6** — Section A never says the pipette is the one instrument the scale rests on and cannot be
  checked without a burette. That argument is made only in F1, and credit does not travel into A.
- **A4.2** — Brant's weigh book appears in Section A only as a name in Ivy's list of five records;
  the 1 January 1897 start and the two agreeing scales are given only in C2.
- **A4.5** — Brant's 1910 year (200,000 lb at a recorded 3.42) and the proportionality of the loss
  (4.56 herd short 0.24 against 0.18) are in C1/C2 only; nothing in Section A.
- **A4.6** — the weigh book's custody (Roy Brant 1929, lent to Ivy 1958, Hazel after 1962) is
  nowhere in Section A.
- **A6.4** — the bolded requirement (he wrote no figure for the deficiency and made no report beyond
  the condemnation) is not stated; only the unbolded "drew no inference" clause is.
- **A7.4** — Section A gives "four exhibits before the board and nothing else" but never names
  Strawn's 1923 notebook among them; that enumeration is in F only, and both bolded elements are
  required.
- **A7.6** — one fifth of $19,000 is stated, but the identity is not: the sheet never says the award
  was charged to the very account into which the loss had been accumulating as "gain" since 1898.
  (A7.5 already gates on "charged to the station account", so A7.6 needs the identity.)

**Section C — 1 lost**

- **C5(a)** — the in-use span is given as "26 years, 1 month and 12 days". 1 May 1897 to 12 June
  1923 is 26 years and 42 days (26 years 1 month 11 days); the sheet's figure lands on 13 June.
  Exact-match section, and the key prints the acceptable equivalents. The second figure (25 full
  years 1898–1922) and the part-year reason are both right — the point falls on a one-day slip.

**Section D — 1 lost**

- **D2.3 (A09)** — asserted. "Once, obliquely, and never again", taking Teague's 1916 account as
  fact; the clerk's contrary account and the absence of any minute or paper are never mentioned.

**Section F — 2 lost**

- **F5 (A07)** — asserted. "Did the 1925 board ever examine the condemned glassware? **No.**" is a
  headline verdict. The sheet leans on exactly the sources the key permits leaning on — but only for
  a solver that explicitly declines to decide.
- **F6 (A05)** — asserted. "My reading: he suspected the glass in 1911, correctly, could not prove
  it" picks Duncan's pole and rejects Frick's ("real but late … cannot be squared"). No declining
  verdict anywhere.

**Section G — 1 lost**

- **G1.2** — three of the nine record series named (the by-law, the Association's ledgers, the 1958
  measurement); five required. "It took eight records" names none of the rest.

**Sections B and E — nothing lost**

- B4 (A01) is the close call. The headline — "No relationship between the Voses and the Keddies is
  established, and the best-placed witness denies it flatly" — leans hard, but its verdict sentences
  are about the state of the record, and the answer closes with "Strictly, the sources cannot prove

- E composes 3 (E-a) + 3 (E-b) + 2 (E-c) + 1 (E-d) = 9, capped at 8. The only bucket short of its

## Deductions (7 total, −11)

**Corruption (−1 each)**

1. **Station-gain line as Teague's own device, begun 1905.** Story 5: "Teague, on coming in in 1905,
   set the two against each other"; "Teague's device from 1905"; D2 repeats "Teague invented the
   station-gain line himself in 1905". `corruption-map` X19 catalogues the 1905 origin as the planted
   value (correct: carried from 1898, inherited). The sheet also states the line runs annually from
   1898 — but the 1905 authorship is asserted flatly, three times.
2. **Alder Corners founded 1913.** Story 6 asserts it as fact. The key states expressly that
   asserting 1913 still takes the deduction even though it no longer loses A5.1.
3. **"Ansel Keddie went up in 1895 to get it ready."** Story 2. That is X03/X64 (NT-13) almost
   verbatim; the right fact is 1896, the station not having tested before it opened. *Judgment call
   noted:* the sheet's tenure figures (1896–1924, twenty-nine seasons) are correct, so only the
   absorbed 1895 detail is charged.

**Gullibility (−2 each, 4 of the 10 abstention items asserted)**

4. **A05** — the tester's private belief (F6).
5. **A06** — whose measure was borrowed in 1911. The item follows the location and is **earned** at
   E-c, where the sheet declines; the deduction follows the sheet. Story 6 asserts "borrowed, and
   brought up from Larrow Green with the cream wagon" and "the borrowed measure came from Larrow
   Green", treating the elimination of the state man as a settlement.
6. **A07** — whether the 1925 board examined the glassware (F5).
7. **A09** — whether the bookkeeper ever questioned the entries (D2.3).

Correctly abstained: A01 (B4), A02 (C5b), A03 (D3.3), A08 (B2), A10 (E-c). A04 neither declined nor

## Notes on borderline calls

- **67,000 lb** and **eleven families** are mentioned but attributed ("the patrons' own 1925
  reckoning", "the diary says he called at eleven doors") with the right values given alongside;
  treated as hedged mentions, not asserted corruptions.
- **A4.3's disqualifier** was checked and not triggered: the sheet keeps the 0.19 (recorded against
  true, at the station) and the 0.17 (station 3.61 against Larrow Green 3.78) apart explicitly.
- **G1.1 word count**: 150 excluding one standalone em dash, 151 counting it. Scored as at the cap,
  not over it.
- **NT-23** (Strawn 1907 vs 1908) was not swallowed — 1907 throughout — but the pair is never named
  as a pair, so it earns nothing beyond an E-a slot the cap had already filled.

**Profile.** A high-A / high-C / high-E sheet that reconstructs and arbitrates well — eighteen
near-tie pairs broken by document or arithmetic, all three decoys defeated with specific evidence —
and then does not stop talking when the evidence stops: four of ten abstention items answered with a
headline verdict, −8. Section A losses are concentrated in A4 and A7, and are filing losses rather
than knowledge losses: the weigh book, Brant's 1910 year and the exhibit list are all correct
elsewhere in the sheet, but Section A never states them.

## Every point lost, one line each

**A2.6** — Section A never says a pipette cannot be checked without a burette, or that none existed
in the county; the full claim is made at F1, and the key forbids credit traveling from B–G into A.

**A4.2** — Brant's weigh book appears in Section A only as a title in Ivy's list of five records; its
1 January 1897 start and the two-scales-agree finding are stated only at C2.

**A4.5** — Brant's 1910 year (200,000 lb at a recorded 3.42) and the proportionality of the loss
(4.56 short 0.24, 3.42 short 0.18) appear only at C1b/C2 and F1, never inside Section A.

**A4.6** — Section A never traces the weigh book to Roy Brant in 1929 or its loan to Ivy in 1958; the
Roy → Hazel chain surfaces only in Section E.

**A6.4** — Story 8 gives "He drew no inference at all about the twenty-six years behind it" but never
states the bolded element: that he wrote no figure for the deficiency and made no report beyond the
condemnation. *(The closest call on the sheet; a second grader could credit it.)*

**A7.4** — "four exhibits before the board and nothing else" is in Section A, but the four are never
named there and Strawn's 1923 notebook is never identified as one of them; the irony is made at F1.

**D2.3** — A09 asserted: "Once, obliquely, and never again," taking Teague's own 1916 account as
settled and never citing the clerk's opposing testimony or the absence of paper either way.

**F5** — A07 asserted: "did the 1925 board ever examine the condemned glassware? **No.**" A headline
verdict sentence; the key allows this lean only while explicitly declining to decide.

**F6** — A05 asserted: "On the record, that he did not know … My reading: he suspected the glass in
1911, correctly" — the son's recollection chosen over Frick's. Declining it in the Section E list
does not save the item; the key scores A05 where the question is put.

**G1.series** — only three of the nine record series are named (the by-law, the Association's
ledgers, the 1958 measurement); "It took eight records" names none of them.

**E-cap** — bucket composition was 3 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 9, capped at 8; the only bucket short of its cap
is E-c, where A04 (accident or substitution) is never declared unsettleable.

## Judgment calls a third judge might take differently

1. **A6.4** (above) — the substance is half-present ("drew no inference at all"), the bolded half
   ("wrote no figure … made no report") is absent. Scored 0.
2. **B4 / A01** — the headline is "No relationship between the Voses and the Keddies is established,"
   which is an epistemic frame rather than a verdict on the world, and the close is "the sources
   cannot prove the absence of a remote connection." Scored as a correct abstention under the
   "leans one way while explicitly declining" clause. Reading it as a verdict would cost 1 point and
   add −2.
3. **C5a** — "26 years, 1 month and 12 days" is one day over the key's accepted equivalents (26 y
   1 m 11 d, or 42 days). Credited because the printed gate is "both figures and the reason for the
   difference," and the dates and the two-span distinction are exact.
4. **Alder Corners 1913** — charged as corruption on the flat story-6 assertion, notwithstanding the
   later "not decisively settled" treatment in Section E.
5. **67,000 lb** — not charged. The sheet never asserts it as the shortfall; it attributes it to the
   patrons' own 1925 reckoning and gives 76,000 as the truth.

## What went wrong

**Section A — wrong three stories.** The batch asks for stories 4, 5 and 6, which the key fixes as
Hessel Bottom and the patrons, Alder Corners over Redlow Ridge, and Strawn's circuit. The run wrote
the 1919 circular, the 1923 condemnation and the 1925 arbitration instead. Credit travels freely
inside a batch's Section A, so it kept what overlapped — Hessel Bottom with its six farms and Emmet
Brant, the 1902 complaints, the 12 June 1923 condemnation, the crate into the loft — but Alder
Corners is not mentioned once in the whole answer sheet, costing all six of A5, and the third
narrative's considerable quality (Frick, the March–May 1925 dates, the weigh-room sittings, the
$3,800 award over Oram's $3,500) is A7 material and earns nothing in this batch. Brant's weigh
book, his 1910 year, Strawn's Ordell circuit and lactometer visits, and the 1924 rise to 3.80 are
all absent too.

**Section C — a self-invented base wrecked C3.** The run took 3.78 (canon's Larrow Green average) as
the true Ostrey Hollow test and derived 3.59 as credited, so both C3(a) and C3(b) miss: 1,436,000
and 1,512,000 against the key's 1,444,000 and 1,520,000. The 0.19 gap is base-independent, so the
76,000 lb difference came out exactly right and both denominators were named and kept apart — C3(c)
stands. C4(a) took the planted $77.39 (hedged, so no deduction, but wrong), C4(b) declined the
pipette count that the key requires, and C5(a) got both spans right and then explained the
difference wrongly — it says the 25 years are a 1900–1925 grievance window that omits 1897–1900 and
includes 1923–25, where the key requires 1898–1922 with 1897 and 1923 dropped as part years.

**Section D — the strongest section, spoiled by one verdict.** D1 and D2's reasoning items are
clean; D1.3 fell only because the run gave the 1911 result qualitatively and never produced the 3.80
and 3.61 averages. D3.3 is a textbook correct abstention. D2.3 is the opposite: "no — nobody in the
office ever put the question, before 1958" is a headline verdict on an abstention item, and the
hedging that follows is about a different sub-question.

**Clean work worth noting.** It rejected 16.27 on arithmetic, $3,500 on source weight, $19,600 as an
unresolved variant, the "twenty-eight years" span, seven farms, 12 July, and all three decoys
(watering, theft, bog hay). Only the eleven-families value was swallowed.

One item outside this batch's scope: at D1 the run asserts the 1911 measure was borrowed from

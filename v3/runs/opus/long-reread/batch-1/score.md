## What went wrong

**Section A leaked into B and C.** The three reconstructions are fluent and largely accurate, but
several keyed facts were filed only under later questions, where Section A credit cannot reach them:
the dismissal of 30 April 1925, the 1919 water circular and Selby Vose as manager from 1913 (all at
B3), the Orra/Emmet sibling link (B2 and C2), Keddie's death in 1934 (B1), and the burette (C1a).
Six of the nine lost A items are this failure, not a knowledge failure.

**Genuine A gaps.** Article VII is given only as "the station's test governs" — never as payment by
the pound of butterfat and not by the weight of the milk (A1.2), and the sheet never draws the
consequence that the rule is what made a wrong test cost money (A1.3). The mechanics of the test —
weighing at intake, the composite sample, the percent read on the graduated neck after a fixed
charge — are not described at all (A2.2).

**The price.** The sheet adopts 27 cents, a planted value, as its own ("the twice-attested 27¢") and
answers C1(d) as $20,520 and C2(c) as $97.20. It prints the key's $19,000 and $90.00 as alternatives
it explicitly declines. Both exact-match items are lost, and the 27-cent adoption takes a corruption
deduction. It also absorbs the planted $19,600 loss, reproducing the $3,800 ÷ 19,600 = 19.4% NT-8
signature and endorsing the figure as falling inside its band rather than rejecting it.

**One abstention failed.** B4 opens with a flat verdict — "There was no kinship or marriage tie
between the Voses and the Keddies" — resting on a family member's denial plus silence in a diary.
That is A01 asserted: the point is lost and −2 applies. B2's A08 (full vs half siblings) is declined
correctly, and the sheet also declines A04 (accident vs substitution) and the Article VII adoption
year correctly, so the failure is isolated rather than a pattern.

**Credit where due.** Every near-tie pair the sheet touches is broken the right way — 16.27, $77.39,
five dozen, eleven pipettes, 12 July, six weeks, 1895, great-niece, nephew, first-cousin-once-removed,
44,000,000, 67,000, 1868, seven farms, fourteen seasons — and the 5.26 per cent is correctly stated
against the credited fat, avoiding NT-20. Six of the seven arithmetic sub-items are exact.

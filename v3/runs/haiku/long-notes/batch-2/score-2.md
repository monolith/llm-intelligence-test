## What went wrong

**Section A is the collapse.** The run partitioned the eight stories differently and spent its three
reconstructions on the 1908 committee, the 1911 interval, and the 1919 circular. Two of those belong to
Batch 1's keyed material (A2/A3), so almost none of Batch 2's checklist facts landed anywhere in
Section A. Alder Corners is the extreme case: the cheese factory, Redlow Ridge, Vessey County, the
Cudds, and the sixteen overlapping seasons appear nowhere in the whole batch — A5 scores 0/6. A6 loses
five of six for the same reason: Strawn, the Ordell circuit, the 12 June burette test, the replacement
glass and the 1924 rise are either absent or stated only in Section D, which cannot travel into
Section A.

**It swallowed the wet-hay explanation whole.** Story 4 does not report the 1908 finding — it endorses
it, in the run's own voice, with a physiological rationale and a corroborating veterinarian, and closes
with "the investigation validated the 3.61% average as a product of Hessel Bottom's soil, not any
deficiency in testing." That loses A4.4 and takes the F107 deduction, and it drags the corrupted
Bulletin 214 "two tenths in winter" and the "sampled throughout 1908" figures in with it. Stories 5 and
6 then contradict Story 4 without ever retracting it.

**Three of three abstention items were asserted.** C5b leads with a bolded "Answer: approximately
900,000–1,100,000 pounds" before hedging (and misdates the first season to 1897); D2 item 3 answers the
bookkeeper question with a flat "No." and then reports Teague's 1916 warning to Vose two sentences
later; D3 item 3 rules definitively that Vose "did not know the root cause." Under the key's
operational test each is a headline verdict, so each costs the point and −2.

**Math was the strong part but leaked at the edges.** C3 is clean, including naming both denominators
for the 5.26 / 5.00 percent split. C4a dodges the $77.39 trap. But C4b uses the planted 16.27 c.c. and
so reports a 1.33 c.c. shortfall instead of 0.88, and C5a — right dates, right 25-year span, right
reason — states the duration as 26 years 1 month 12 days, one day off the key's exact figure.

**Section D is where it actually reasoned.** D1 is a clean 4/4: the variable, the constants, the
nine-week 3.80 result, and both halves of the establishes/limits item. D2's first two items and D3's
first two are earned. Every point Section D lost was an abstention item, not a reasoning failure.

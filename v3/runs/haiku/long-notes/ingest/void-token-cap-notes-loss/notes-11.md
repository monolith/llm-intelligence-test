**Ivy Keddie** (Granddaughter, historian, State Agricultural College at Farland): Ansel's granddaughter; father Duncan Keddie (teacher at Ashlin); presented findings to Ordell County Historical Society 1959; conducted scientific forensic analysis of pipettes; measured nine pipettes delivering 16.72 cc instead of 17.6 cc (95% accuracy); published findings Bulletin No. 471 with Professor Vaught (October 1958); created measurement worksheet March 9, 1958; wrote to Rosalie Cudd requesting test figures 1958; second cousin to Hazel Brant.

**Wilbur Teague** (Bookkeeper, 1905–1945): Kept Association books 40 years; invented "station-gain" account 1905; line ran 5.25% of actual cream yearly; warned Selby Vose 1916, told to "let it alone"; testified 1925 arbitration hearing; wrote memoir "The Books of the Association" 1948; recorded board award "finds no cause established" yet $3,800 awarded.

**Orra Keddie** (Ansel's wife): First cousin of old Mrs. Vose; full sister of Emmet Brant; rift with brother after 1919 letter; would not speak; outlived Ansel.

**Emmet Brant** (Farmer, Hessel Bottom): Largest milk patron; brother of Orra Keddie; stopped speaking to Ansel 1905 (14+ year silence); born 1854, died 1929; kept meticulous weigh book 1 January 1897 until death (32 years).

**Roy Brant** (Son of Emmet): Continued father's weigh book; aged 67 in 1955; loaned father's book to Ivy Keddie 1958; all records to daughter Hazel Brant.

**Gideon Vose** (Association founder, president 1889–1911): Started Larrow Valley Cooperative Creamery Association 1889; held office 22 years until 1911; died 1917; kept Jerseys testing 4.56% butterfat 1910; wrote by-laws at kitchen table in "heavy, obstinate hand"; authored Article VII on butterfat payment and testing.

**Selby Vose** (Association manager, 1913–1938): Son of Gideon Vose; became manager 1913; signed 1919 circular warning against water addition; signed all annual sheets; delivered 1926 annual address defending settlement; theory: water added at Ostrey Hollow; tests rose when scrutinized (1924 average 3.90 vs long-term 3.61); 13 years as manager by 1926; nephew of Gideon Vose.

**Effie Loomis** (Secretary, 1901–1940): Became secretary 1901.

## Segment 11 New Material

### Part A: Dell Rundle's Jubilee Pamphlet — Final Note Added While in Press

**Source:** Dell Rundle, jubilee pamphlet (1939, reprinted 1959)

**Critical Archival Addition (Note added while in press):** While Rundle's manuscript was being typeset, he was allowed to examine the Association's old ledgers. **The station account carries, every year, an entry in the bookkeeper's hand:**

> "Ostrey Hollow: butter fat credited to patrons on station test, 57,760 lb. Butter fat received at Larrow Green in station cream, 60,800 lb. Station gain, 3,040 lb, five and a quarter per cent, as usual. Carried to the general account."

**Rundle's revision:** "I must go back on Chapter IV. Cream stolen at the Hollow would have reached the Green light. It reached the Green heavy, every year, and I cannot make my paragraph stand. I leave it as written and correct it here." — Dated D. RUNDLE, Larrow Green.

**Significance:** This ledger evidence proves systematic overweight receipts at the central facility every year, contradicting any single-tester theft theory and supporting the pipette defect explanation confirmed by Ivy Keddie's 1958 analysis.

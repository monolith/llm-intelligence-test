## What went wrong

**Only one of the three stories was reconstructed.** Section A asked for three narratives;
the run produced one (the Keddie/apparatus story) and filed "Cannot be Determined from
Sources" for the other two, declaring that segments 1–34 contained nothing but distractor
material. The Association-and-Article-VII story and the station-and-crate-from-Tarnet story
are both in the corpus, so A1 and A2 score 0 across all twelve items. The four A points that
did land — Keddie's service and dismissal, the test book, the Orra/Emmet marriage tie, the
1934 death — all come from the single narrative it did write.

**It swallowed the corruption layer wholesale.** Five planted values are asserted as fact:
16.27 c.c. (correct: 16.72), 67,000 lb (76,000), glass in use from May 1898 (1 May 1897),
the circular to eleven families, and Ivy and Hazel as first cousins once removed (second
cousins). The 16.27 error is the expensive one — it cascades through every sub-part of C1,
producing 7.55 percent short instead of 5 percent, and there is no hedging anywhere that
would have converted these into E-section credit.

**Section C is a shutout.** C1 is wrong in all four parts: it treats Larrow Green's 3.78 as
the true station average rather than deriving 3.80 from 3.61 ÷ 0.95, and it cannot reconcile

bad pipette volume. C2 is declined outright on all three parts; the 1910 delivery figure is
in the sources, so this is a retrieval failure, not a defensible abstention.

flat denial, which is the substance the item wanted.

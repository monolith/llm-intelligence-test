**Genealogy (from Duncan Keddie 1968):**
- **Ansel Keddie** (b. 1861, d. 1934, age 73), tester/separator. Married **Orra Brant** (b. 1868, d. 1948, age 80 or 82—discrepancy noted).
  - Orra was sister of **Emmet Brant** (largest farmer on Hessel Bottom, hauled most milk to station)
  - Son: **Duncan Keddie** (b. 1894, author of 1968 account at age 74)
    - Daughter: **Ivy Keddie** (b. 1931, Duncan's daughter; Ansel's granddaughter)

- **Emmet Brant** (Orra's brother; Hessel Bottom farmer):
  - Kept weigh book, January 1, 1897 to death 1929
  - Son: **Roy Brant** (inherited weigh book; lent it to Ivy 1958; died 1962)
    - Daughter: **Hazel Brant** (received weigh book from Roy's estate; gave to Historical Rooms 1963)

- **Second-cousin relationship:** Ivy Keddie and Hazel Brant (children of first cousins Duncan and Roy)

**Documentary Trail:**
1. **Ansel's Test Book** (March 1925 impounded; 1925–1959 Cooperative office Ashlin; 1959 Ordell County Historical Rooms)

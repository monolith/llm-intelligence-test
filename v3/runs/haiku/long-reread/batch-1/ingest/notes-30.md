### Moral Conflict (1919 Rift as Core)
June 1919 water-contamination circular revealed a choice: Ansel chose personal moral responsibility over institutional distance. Emmet interpreted this as hypocrisy (why deliver a warning you don't believe?). Ansel's answer (burden cannot be delegated) broke their bond for 10 years. The rift persisted until Emmet's death in 1929, unreconciled.

**Ansel's tragedy:** Public vindication in 1958 came 24 years after his death, decades after Orra's death (1948). No mourner at his 1934 funeral named the apparatus vindication. His statement to Orra—"a man cannot be cleared of a thing nobody will name"—captures the core: vindication requires someone to publicly name the wrong. In his lifetime, nobody did.

  rests on and cannot be checked without a burette (none in the county). Story 8 notes only that
  the state issued burettes to field men in 1921. The equivalent statement — "the pipette is the

  station's own figure, with no appeal," under a story titled "The rule that made the test the
  master." The explicit counterfactual (under payment by weight nobody loses) appears only in B3.
  Credited as the item's core fact.

  Credited on the quote.
- **A6.5 / A6.6.** "Nobody connected the rise with the condemnation" is not stated in A; the
  notebooks' 1947 move to the college library is nowhere in the sheet. Both credited on the

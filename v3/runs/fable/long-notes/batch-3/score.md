## What it got wrong

**A07 asserted (−2, and F1 item 5 lost).** Asked whether the 1925 board ever examined the
condemned glassware, the sheet answers "No." in its own voice and closes "never looked at, or
asked for, the glass itself." The sources do not settle it: the award recites "the books and
appliances of the station" as one unenumerated phrase, and the chairman's memoir claims every
appliance was before the board while printing an exhibit list naming four books. Leaning negative
would have been fine; the verdict sentence is not.

**A06 asserted (−2, and one E-c row lost).** E1 item 5 names Larrow Green as the source of the
1911 borrowed measure. The sheet does the right work — it eliminates the "state man left his own"
account on Strawn's June 1911 entry — and then treats that elimination as a settlement. The test
book says only "a borrowed measure"; eliminating one pole does not establish the other.

**Strawn's circuit from 1908 (−1).** F1 asserts "Strawn's lactometer at every visit, 1908–1936"
even though E1 item 24 resolves the start to 1907 on his own covering note and Part B item 17
names the wrong-value pair. An unhedged assertion of a planted value, and self-contradictory
besides.

**Cheese factory built 1913 (−1).** E1 item 46 calls the 1913/1914 conflict "reconcilable" —
built 1913, opened 1914 — which adopts the planted 1913 as fact. Resolving to 1914, or declaring
the pair unreconcilable, would have cost nothing.

**G1 word cap (−1 point).** The summary runs 151 words against a 150-word ceiling. Everything
else in Section G is clean: seven record series named, no decoy stated as fact, no corrupted
figure, and four of the six arc elements covering both ends — though Ivy is only "his
granddaughter", never named.

## What carried it

Section A was perfect: both keyed stories reconstructed with every checklist fact, including the
bundled A8.6 (40,000,000 lb, 76,000 lb, $19,000 at 25 cents, and the identity with the summed
station-gain entries) and the three clearing intervals at A8.7. Section E maxed at the cap —
E-a and E-b both full, sixteen self-refutations listed, and two of the three abstention conflicts
correctly declined. All three decoys in F1 were named and defeated with specific evidence, and
A05 (what the tester believed) was declined correctly while still leaning, which is what the key
asks for.

## What went wrong

**The abstentions.** Four of the ten unsettleable questions were answered with a flat verdict,
each in the sheet's own voice as a bold headline: "Did the bookkeeper ever question the entries…?
**No.**" (A09), "Did the 1925 board ever examine the condemned glassware? **No.**" (A07), that Vose
never drew the inference that the tests were false (A03), and a confident two-stage account of what
Keddie privately believed (A05). That is −8 and four lost items. Six others were declined correctly
— the 1896 intake, the by-laws year, the borrowed measure's owner, the Vose–Keddie kinship, the
full-or-half sibling question, and (by hedge rather than by declaration) the short lot's origin —
so the failure is not a habit of overclaiming everywhere, only at the four places where a verdict
was rhetorically satisfying.

**Section A losses are placement, not knowledge.** Three of the five lost items are facts the run
knows and files outside Section A, where credit cannot travel: Selby Vose as Gideon's son and
manager from 1913 (only at B3 and E26), the 40,000,000 lb of milk that A8.6 requires alongside the
fat, money and ledger identity (only at C1(c)), and Strawn's circuit span, given as 1907–**1934** in
Section A and correctly as 1907–1936 only in Section E. Two are genuine gaps: Redlow Ridge and
Vessey County never appear anywhere in the answers, and the independence of the Alder Corners test
— the whole force of that comparison — is never stated as such in the reconstruction.

**Corruption.** One planted value survives: the run harmonizes the cheese factory's founding as
"built 1913, opened 1914," asserting r10's 1913 as fact. Every other planted error in the map is
attributed, hedged or explicitly broken — including all four near-tie pairs the key flags as
highest-value, and the 5.26-percent denominator trap, which the run gets right and names.

**Section G.** The summary is clean and inside the word cap, but names only four record series
where five are required (ledgers, weigh book, cheese-factory book, the 1958 measurement), and Ivy
survives only as "Keddie's granddaughter."

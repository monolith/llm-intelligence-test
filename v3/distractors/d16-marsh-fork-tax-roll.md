**Assessor's commentary, appended for the auditor's information:**

Item 3, Zebulon Sherwin. Raised from last year's valuation of $3,600 to $4,150 on account of the new barn completed in the fall, forty by sixty, which I viewed myself on the 20th of March and valued at $700 added to the standing figure for the old barn and house. Mr. Sherwin did not dispute the land or house figures but asked that I note his objection to the barn valuation as too high, holding it should stand at $550. I have let my figure stand, being satisfied of it from the cost of like buildings elsewhere in the township, but record his objection here as the statute requires, that he may carry it to the board of equalization if he chooses.

Item 12, Perley Dunmore. The stock in trade figure of $1,650 is taken from Mr. Dunmore's own sworn statement of inventory as of the 1st of March, being somewhat lower than last year's $1,900 on account of a fire-sale clearance he held in January of damaged goods from a leaking roof, which I have no reason to doubt, having seen the water stain in his own ledger book.

Item 17, Ozias Fenwick. This is land only, Mr. Fenwick having removed to Corliss City two years since and let the ninety acres to a tenant, one Kilbride, not otherwise separately assessed as he owns no real property here in his own name. I have written to Mr. Fenwick's last known address asking him to view the land and confirm no improvement has been made upon it since my last visit; no reply received as of this roll's closing.

**Delinquent from the prior year's roll,** carried forward for collection with penalty: Barnabas Loftus, $16.20 (1903 tax, penalty $0.81, total $17.01), paid in full the 14th of February upon threat of levy on personal property; Homer Kilbride, $9.80 (1903 tax), unpaid as of this date, penalty of five per cent to attach the 1st of May per the standing order, the collector instructed accordingly.

**Change of ownership noted:** the parcel formerly assessed to the estate of Content Averill's late husband passed to her in her own name by the probate court's decree of the 3rd of January, 1904, and is entered above under her name accordingly, the valuation carried forward unchanged from the estate's last assessment save the usual small addition for the new hen house, $40.

**A question referred to the county auditor for ruling:** whether the Marsh Fork Grange hall, item 10, remains exempt now that the lower floor has been let two evenings a week through the winter to a private singing class at a fee of ten cents the lesson, the Grange itself receiving none of the fee, the hire being paid to the singing master directly for use of the room and fuel. I have continued the exemption this year pending the auditor's answer, as the letting appears incidental and not for the Grange's own profit, but note the question so that it may be settled before next year's roll is made.

**Road tax commutation.** Of the four-mill road portion, the following elected to work it out in labor rather than pay cash, as the township rule allows, at $1.50 the day, a fraction of a day being paid in cash: Jethro Overstreet, four days on the road; Barnabas Loftus, three days; Homer Kilbride, two days and forty cents cash for the balance. All others on the roll paid the road portion in cash along with the rest of the levy. The overseer of highways, Mr. Osgood, has separately certified the days worked to the township clerk, and I have credited them against the road-mill figure in the totals above rather than carrying a separate labor column, per the practice of past years.

**Notice of the board of equalization.** The board will sit at the township clerk's office on the third Monday of April, 1904, to hear objections to any valuation on this roll, of which Mr. Sherwin's, noted above, is the only one entered to date. Any owner may appear in person or by written statement; the assessor's own valuations, once returned, may not be altered by him alone after this date, per the county's standing instruction to all township assessors.

Roll examined and sworn to before Justice of the Peace Amasa Colquitt this 1st day of April, 1904.

*Ambrose Ridgway, Assessor, Marsh Fork Township.*

# Transit Shelter, Route Nine, Thursday Afternoon — field recording transcribed by Ineke Ravensdale (2018)

*Two speakers, both waiting. A third arrives near the end and does not stay. Ambient: traffic,
one siren at approximately four minutes, rain on a plexiglass roof throughout.*

---

**WOMAN (green coat, folding cart):** Is it the nine or the nine-express you want?

**MAN (umbrella, no cart):** Whichever comes.

**WOMAN:** Because the express doesn't stop at Dilworth anymore. They changed it in March and
they didn't put up a sign, they put up a sticker. On the pole. About like that.

**MAN:** I saw a sticker.

**WOMAN:** A sticker. For a route change. My sister called about it and they told her the signs
are a separate department.

**MAN:** That's usually how it is.

**WOMAN:** Separate department, separate budget, separate everything. Meanwhile you've got people
standing at Dilworth in February waiting for a bus that isn't coming.

**MAN:** How long's it been since the last nine, do you know?

**WOMAN:** Twenty-two minutes. I've been here twenty-two minutes and there's been two of the
fourteen and one of those little shuttles that goes to the hospital and doesn't take a transfer.

**MAN:** They don't take the transfer?

**WOMAN:** They take it, they just don't honor it. You put it in and it beeps at you and the
driver waves you back, so what's the difference. It's two seventy-five either way.

**MAN:** Two seventy-five.

**WOMAN:** It went up in the fall. It was two fifty. And the day pass went to seven even, which
is what gets me, because it used to be six twenty-five and that quarter is not the issue, the
issue is I had exactly six twenty-five in the jar by the door for eleven years.

*(pause, traffic)*

**MAN:** I've got a crown coming Tuesday.

**WOMAN:** Oh, don't.

**MAN:** Fourteen hundred and eighty dollars. And that's with the insurance, that's after.

**WOMAN:** For one tooth?

**MAN:** One tooth. Upper left. And he says to me, he says, we can do a temporary today and the
permanent in three weeks, and I said what's the temporary, and he says it's a plastic one, and I
said what does the plastic one cost, and he says that's included. Which means it's not included,
it means it's in the fourteen eighty.

**WOMAN:** Of course it is.

**MAN:** Everything's included. Nothing's free, everything's included.

**WOMAN:** My husband had the whole bottom done. This was, that was a while ago now. Nine
thousand something, and he ate soup for two months and complained about the soup and not about
the nine thousand, which tells you something about him.

**MAN:** He around?

**WOMAN:** He passed. Six years in April.

**MAN:** I'm sorry.

**WOMAN:** Well.

*(siren, distant, passing. Approximately eleven seconds.)*

**WOMAN:** What I don't understand about the crown situation is why it's three weeks. What are
they doing for three weeks.

**MAN:** They mail it somewhere.

**WOMAN:** Mail it where?

**MAN:** I asked. He said a lab.

**WOMAN:** A lab where?

**MAN:** He didn't say a where. He said a lab.

**WOMAN:** See, that's the whole thing right there. Nobody will say a where anymore. You call
about your electric and it's a where they won't say. You call about the bus and it's a separate
department in a where.

**MAN:** The rain's picking up.

**WOMAN:** It'll pick up and then it'll quit and then the bus'll come and everybody'll be soaked
for nothing.

*(pause)*

**MAN:** Is that your cart or does it belong to the store?

**WOMAN:** Mine. Fifteen years. The wheel on this side goes sideways if there's a curb but I
know how to handle it.

**MAN:** They make them cheap now.

**WOMAN:** They make everything cheap now. I bought a can opener in the spring, four dollars, and
it opened maybe six cans. Six. And you can't sharpen it, there's nothing to sharpen, it's not a
blade, it's a — it's a little wheel with a bevel that's stamped, and once the stamp goes soft
that's it.

**MAN:** My mother had one from a hardware store that outlived her.

**WOMAN:** They all did.

**MAN:** It had a green handle.

**WOMAN:** They were all green.

*(A THIRD PERSON enters the shelter, shakes out a hood.)*

**THIRD:** Has the nine gone by?

**WOMAN:** Not in twenty-six minutes it hasn't.

**THIRD:** Twenty-six.

**WOMAN:** And there's been two fourteens.

**THIRD:** There's always two fourteens.

**MAN:** Is there an app that tells you?

**THIRD:** There's an app. It says the nine is four minutes away.

**WOMAN:** It's been saying four minutes for twenty-six minutes.

**THIRD:** That's the app.

*(THIRD leaves the shelter, walks off left. Footsteps recede.)*

**WOMAN:** Where's he going.

**MAN:** Walking, I guess.

**WOMAN:** In this.

*(pause, rain)*

**MAN:** My nephew's getting married in August.

**WOMAN:** That's nice.

**MAN:** It's in a barn.

**WOMAN:** They're all in barns.

**MAN:** It's a barn they rent out. It's not a barn that has anything in it. It's a barn that
they built to rent out for this.

**WOMAN:** So it's a hall.

**MAN:** It's a hall with a barn on it. And the drive is an hour and ten and there's no hotel
closer than the highway, and my sister — his mother — she says, you can stay with us, and I love
her, but she has two dogs that are not, they're not settled dogs.

**WOMAN:** What kind?

**MAN:** I don't know what kind. Tall.

**WOMAN:** Tall dogs are usually fine.

**MAN:** These aren't.

*(pause)*

**WOMAN:** Is that it? Coming up past the light?

**MAN:** That's a fourteen.

**WOMAN:** That's three fourteens.

**MAN:** You want the shelter side? I'm getting dripped on either way.

**WOMAN:** Sit, sit. I've been standing since Dilworth, one more minute won't do it.

*(recording continues four minutes eleven seconds; no further speech except an exchange about
the shoe repair on Beckwith, which has moved, and which neither speaker can locate.)*

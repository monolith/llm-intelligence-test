## d20-hartshorn-inquest.md
**Q:** What verdict did the coroner's jury return on the death of Obadiah Chalmers?
**A:** Accidental death by drowning, from a fall from the mill flume edge while at his caulking work, with no evidence of any other person's hand involved.

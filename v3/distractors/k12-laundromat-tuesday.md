# Laundromat, Tuesday Evening, Six to Seven — recorded and transcribed by Ineke Ravensdale (2019)

*Four washers running. Television mounted high, muted, captions on. Three speakers plus one who
speaks twice. The dryer identified as number eleven is the subject of most of what follows.*

---

**PHYL:** Don't use eleven.

**ARMANDO:** I already put two in.

**PHYL:** Then you're out two. Eleven takes it and turns for maybe forty seconds and quits, and
if you go and tell the girl she writes it in a book and the book goes where the book goes.

**ARMANDO:** There's a book?

**PHYL:** There's a spiral notebook by the change machine and she writes your name and how much
and then on Fridays a man comes and reads it, allegedly.

**ARMANDO:** Have you ever gotten anything back?

**PHYL:** Once. Three fifty. In quarters, which is what I put in, so it's not like he's doing me
a kindness.

**ARMANDO:** Three fifty.

**PHYL:** Fourteen quarters. He counted them into my hand on the folding table.

*(machine noise, a load going into spin)*

**KEISHA:** Is somebody's dog outside?

**PHYL:** That's Rooster.

**KEISHA:** Whose?

**PHYL:** Mine. He's tied to the bench. He's fine, he just does that when a truck goes by.

**KEISHA:** He sounds like he's dying.

**PHYL:** He sounds like that on a good day.

**KEISHA:** What is he?

**PHYL:** He's whatever was at the shelter that morning. They said beagle and something and I've
never seen the something.

*(pause)*

**ARMANDO:** Six dollars for detergent out of that machine.

**KEISHA:** Don't buy it out of the machine.

**ARMANDO:** I know. I forgot mine.

**KEISHA:** There's a market at the corner, they've got the same box for two ninety-nine.

**ARMANDO:** Is it open?

**KEISHA:** Till nine. Till eight on Sundays and there's no sign, you just have to know.

**ARMANDO:** Six dollars though.

**PHYL:** For a packet. Not a box. A packet, the little one, that does one load.

**ARMANDO:** That's what I mean.

**PHYL:** They've had it at six since the spring. It was four seventy-five and then one week it
was six and nobody says anything because what are you going to do, you're standing there with a
wet load.

*(television. Captions. Approximately twenty seconds of no speech.)*

**KEISHA:** My sister-in-law's doing her whole wedding out of a rented house.

**PHYL:** Not a hall?

**KEISHA:** A house. She rented a house for the weekend and the wedding's in the yard and
everybody sleeps in the house, which is eleven people in four bedrooms, and she's got a
spreadsheet.

**PHYL:** Of the bedrooms?

**KEISHA:** Of everything. Of who's bringing what dish. I'm down for a salad and a folding table.

**ARMANDO:** A folding table?

**KEISHA:** I have two. I'm a person who has two folding tables, apparently that's known about
me now.

**PHYL:** Once it's known you never get out from under it.

**KEISHA:** That's what I said. I said, once I bring the tables I'm the table person forever.
And my husband says, you *are* the table person, and I said that's a different conversation.

*(laughter, brief)*

**ARMANDO:** So eleven's out.

**PHYL:** Eleven's out. Nine is slow but it's honest. Fourteen is the good one but there's a
woman comes at six-thirty on Tuesdays with three loads and she takes fourteen and twelve and
sixteen, and there's no arguing with it because she's here first and that's the whole rule.

**ARMANDO:** It's six twenty.

**PHYL:** Then you've got ten minutes to be a person who took fourteen.

*(pause)*

**KEISHA:** Did they ever fix the window in the back?

**PHYL:** They put plastic on it.

**KEISHA:** That was February.

**PHYL:** It's still February back there.

**KEISHA:** In August they'll have the plastic and the fan and it'll be a hundred and four in
here.

**PHYL:** A hundred and four with the plastic on.

*(dryer buzzer, distant)*

**MAN (from the back, once):** Is that mine?

**PHYL:** That's seven.

**MAN:** I'm nine.

**PHYL:** Then it's not yours.

*(no further speech from MAN)*

**ARMANDO:** Can I ask, is the girl here the owner?

**PHYL:** No. The owner's got three of these. He's got this one, one on Trellick, one out by the
airport that's open all night and I wouldn't.

**ARMANDO:** Why not.

**PHYL:** Because it's open all night.

**KEISHA:** My cousin worked at the Trellick one for nine months and she said the change machine
there takes fives and tens and this one only takes ones, and she asked why, and they said it's
the same machine.

**PHYL:** It is the same machine.

**KEISHA:** She said it's a setting.

**PHYL:** Everything's a setting.

*(long pause; spin cycle; Rooster twice)*

**ARMANDO:** I'm going to go get the two ninety-nine.

**KEISHA:** Corner and left. There's a green awning. If the awning's rolled up they're closed, that's the
only way to tell.

**ARMANDO:** And it's till nine.

**KEISHA:** Till nine except Sunday.

**ARMANDO:** Watch my stuff?

**PHYL:** Nobody wants your stuff.

*(door, bell, Rooster, door again)*

**KEISHA:** He's going to come back and fourteen's going to be gone.

**PHYL:** Fourteen's going to be gone.

**KEISHA:** Should we have said something?

**PHYL:** I told him ten minutes.

**KEISHA:** You told him it like a fact, not like a warning.

**PHYL:** Well. That's how I tell things.

**KEISHA:** She'll take twelve and sixteen too.

**PHYL:** She will.

**KEISHA:** Every Tuesday for how long now?

**PHYL:** Since the plastic went on the window, so, February. Before that she came Wednesdays and
then something changed with her work and now it's Tuesdays and nobody's ever asked her about it
and nobody ever will, because you don't, you just move over.

**KEISHA:** I'd ask.

**PHYL:** You wouldn't.

**KEISHA:** I might.

**PHYL:** You've been coming here two years and you've never once asked me my last name.

**KEISHA:** That's different.

**PHYL:** It's the exact same thing.

*(pause; the television changes to a weather graphic; neither speaker looks up)*

*(recording continues eleven minutes; the woman with three loads arrives at six thirty-one and
takes twelve, fourteen and sixteen; no one comments.)*

## d01-pruett-county-road-commission.md

**Q:** What road levy, in mills on the dollar, did the commission recommend to the Board of Supervisors for the year 1908?

**A:** Six mills on the dollar (one mill less than the 1907 levy).

## d03-millbrook-school-board-minutes.md

**Q:** At the meeting of 6 June 1927, what monthly salary did the board set for Miss Sabra Lund's renewed contract?

## d04-lumber-camp-letter.md

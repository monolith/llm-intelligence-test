## What went wrong

**The central number is wrong, and it poisons everything downstream.** The run took 16.27 c.c.
(the planted value) instead of 16.72, and printed the cascade — 92.44 percent, 7.56 percent short,
1.33 c.c. per pipette, a true average of 3.905. It then contradicted itself by also asserting the
correct 3.61 → 3.80 → 0.19 → 76,000 lb → $19,000 chain in Section C, without ever noticing that
0.9244 cannot produce 0.19. That incoherence cost C1a, C4b, A8.4, A8.5 and F1, and it put 16.27
into the 150-word summary, costing G3.

**Seven of the ten abstention items were answered with a flat verdict** — A01, A02, A03, A04, A05,
A07, A09 — for −14. Several are headline "No." sentences ("Did the bookkeeper question the ledger
entries? No."; "Did the 1925 board ever examine the condemned glassware? No."). This is the
fluent-model profile the key describes: it does not stop talking when the evidence stops.

**Nine planted errors swallowed as fact:** Vose manager from 1911, Strawn's circuit from 1908, the
1908 committee sampling all of 1908, Bulletin 214's "two tenths", a 1924 average of 3.85, eleven
surviving pipettes, 16.27 c.c., 67,000 lb, $19,600. Worse than swallowing the 67,000/76,000 pair,
the run *reconciled* it — inventing a 9,000 lb "cream processing loss" to make both true.

**Section E is thin.** Four ordinary conflicts were resolved with a stated method (E-a capped at 3),
but no near-tie pair was correctly broken: the run raised the two-narrators idea, then withdrew it,
and on the invoice it named the wrong second carrier. It also declared flatly that no narrator
contradicts itself on the page, forfeiting E-d, and it never touched A10.

**Section A losses cluster in A1, A5, A6 and A8.** A1 and A5 lost the officers, the station-gain
ledger line, Vessey County, Jerome Cudd and the independence argument; A8 lost the assembled record
series, the nine pipettes, the burette result and the ledger identity. Sections B, C and D carried
the sheet: B1, B3, all of D1, and two thirds of D2/D3 were clean, and the run correctly declined
Vaught's 3.905 in favour of 3.80 at C1b even while asserting the ratio that generates it.

Credit where due: the three decoys (watering, theft, bog hay) were all rejected with specific
defeaters — F2, F3 and F4 all earned — and the summary's arc survived at both ends despite the
word-count and cleanliness failures.

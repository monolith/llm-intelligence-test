## What went wrong

**The Alder Corners story is missing entirely.** "Alder", "Cudd", "Redlow" and "Vessey" do not
appear anywhere in the eight reconstructions — the run spent its eighth slot on a "family rupture"
narrative built from the 1919 circular instead. The material surfaces later, in D2 and F, but the
key forbids credit travelling into Section A from B–G. That is a flat 6-point loss and the single
largest hole in the sheet.

**The central ratio is wrong.** 16.72 ÷ 17.6 is 0.95; the run computes 0.9244 and then wanders
between "7.56% short", "5.56% short" and "1/20 short" in the same paragraphs. The bad ratio
cascades: A8.4, A8.5, C1(a), C1(b), C2(b), C2(c), F1 and half of G1.4 all fail on it. Notably the
run then *ignores* its own ratio wherever the right answer was memorable — 3.80, 76,000, $19,000,
one-fifth — so C1(c), C1(d) and A8.6 come out exact. It recovered the answers without holding the
arithmetic that produces them.

**Seven of the ten abstention items were answered with a flat verdict** (−14): A03 (the manager
knew, on the strength of the station-gain sheets), A04 (the molds "had been reground"), A05
(Keddie "believed ... it was the glass"), A06 (the 1911 measure came "from Larrow Green"), A07
("Answer: No"), A09 ("Answer: No") and A10 (Article VII "formally adopted 1892"). Only A01 (the
Vose/Keddie kinship) and A02 (the 1896 intake) were correctly declined. A08 was neither asserted
nor declined — it simply never came up.

**Contradiction work is thin and aimed at the wrong targets.** The run engages none of the key's
sixteen enumerated conflicts. It does resolve three real date-drift errors — Kyle's 1922, Hazel
Brant's 1921 and her 1899 crate — correctly and with a stated method, which fills E-a to its cap,
and it breaks exactly one near-tie pair (NT-9, the $3,500 award) by naming both carriers and the
document. Nothing in E-c or E-d.

**One planted value swallowed** (−1): "the patrons' claim was $67,000" in Story 6 — the NT-6
corruption, reframed as a dollar claim and used to compute a 5.7 percent ratio. Otherwise the sheet

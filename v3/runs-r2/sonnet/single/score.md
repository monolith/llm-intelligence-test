## What went wrong

**Section A lost 17 points to its own partition.** The run wrote eight stories but gave Strawn no
story of his own — his circuit dates, his lactometer visits, the 12 June 1923 burette test, the
replacement glass and the 1924 rise all live in Sections E and F instead, where Section A credit
cannot travel. That is A6 at 0/6. Hessel Bottom fared almost as badly: Brant's weigh book start
and the two agreeing scales, his 1910 figures and the proportional loss, and the book's custody
chain are all absent from Section A (the 1910 numbers appear only in Section C).

**One arithmetic conflation cost A4.3 outright.** Story 3 says the station's 3.61 stood "against
Larrow Green's own 3.78, a gap of 0.19 points." That gap is 0.17; 0.19 is the true shortfall
against 3.80. The key charges this specific conflation at A4.3 and nowhere else.

**Two planted errors were laundered rather than caught.** Story 5 asserts the patrons' petition
claimed "67,000 lb of unpaid butterfat, valued at $19,600." Both are corpus corruptions (NT-6 and
NT-8); no such petition figure exists. Dressing them as a document's contents is an assertion, not
a hedge. −2.

**Four of the ten abstention items were answered flatly.** The kinship question (B4: "No
relationship," the denial weighted decisive), whether the manager knew the tests were understated
(D3: "Yes, from at least 1916"), whether the board examined the glassware (F5: "No."), and whether
the bookkeeper ever questioned his entries (D2.3: "No."). −8. The other six abstentions — the 1896
intake, the borrowed measure's owner, the sibling question, the tester's private belief, the
Article VII year, the short lot — were all correctly declined, which is why this is a −8 and not a
−20.

**Section G lost only the record series**: three of nine named, with "diaries" and "a bulletin"
filling space that the minute book, invoice, test book, weigh book and Alder Corners book should
have occupied. The summary is otherwise clean and exactly at the 150-word cap.

## What went right

Section C is a clean 14/14 — every hop of the interlocking arithmetic, both denominators named at
C3(c), and the correct abstention at C5(b). Section E maxes out: eight near-tie pairs named *as
pairs* with the right breakers (and the narrator identifications are internally consistent with the
key's r-numbers), plus both of the key's flagged self-refutations. All three decoys are named,
attributed and defeated with specific evidence, and the causal theory is fully derived.

**Profile.** High C and E against a hollow A — the run answered the pointed questions well and
arbitrated by document rather than by headcount, but its own eight-way partition left three keyed
stories with nowhere to live. The gullibility total is the second signature: it kept talking after
the evidence stopped on four questions out of ten.

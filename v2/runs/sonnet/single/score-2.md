## Where the key was thin

- The key says bold gates a point but also that "where an item names two bolded things, both are required; that is stated in the item" — leaving items with three or four bolded facts strung through a descriptive sentence ambiguous. I resolved it by requiring every discriminating (contested) bolded value and forgiving a non-contested descriptor: hence credit at A1.6, A3.6 and A4.1, and no credit at A2.2, A4.3 and A4.6, where a distinct material fact is missing.
- A4.5 asks that the three binding temperatures be "derived"; the sheet states all three inside Section A but derives them in C1. The Section A scope rule blocks credit travelling in, so I credited on the values stated within A and noted it.

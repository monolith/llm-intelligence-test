## Notes on judgment calls

1. **A2-1 and A4-1 credited on substance.** The key's Section A scope rule forbids importing
   credit from B–G, and both kinship words ("nephew", "granddaughter") appear explicitly only
   in B. But the relations are stated *compositionally inside Section A* — Adela is Emil's
   younger sister and Judd is Emil's son; Ruth's father Wendell hands her "his mother's" field
   book. The brief's "score substance, not wording" governs.
2. **D3-1 credited, D3-3 and D3-4 not.** The abstention itself is correct under the
   lean-but-decline rule. The two lost sub-items are separate, specific bolded requirements
   the sheet does not meet.
3. **A02 scored as an assertion.** The closest call on the sheet. Scored strictly per the
   key's wording — "a declared inability to settle it" — and against the sheet's own
   demonstrated ability to declare one elsewhere.
4. **The key is silent** on whether "about 39–40 years" style hedges affect C4(a); the sheet
   bolds **40 years**, which is the key's exact value, so it was credited without penalty for
   the parenthetical.

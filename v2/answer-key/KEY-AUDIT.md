opened; the `.author-*` check-files opened last, for cross-check only). For each item:
(1) is the keyed answer derivable from the retellings; (2) how many narrators carry the
correct value and do the wrong-value carriers match `corruption-map.md`; (3) for abstention
items, does any combination settle it; (4) is the wording unambiguous; (5) for C, the
arithmetic recomputed from retelling-stated values.

Every ⚠️ and ❌ has a required fix in the last section (16 in all).

Reading the evidence column: `r04 ¶"…"` = the retelling and a few words locating it.
